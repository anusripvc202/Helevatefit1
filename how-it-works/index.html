Inside the Process

What happens at every stage.

From your first baseline check to your twice-monthly progress reviews, every step is designed so you only have to focus on showing up.

STEP 01 • START

Initial Strength & Body Assessment

knowing your exact starting point.

Before designing your workouts, your coach conducts a structured assessment so nothing is left to guesswork:

  • Muscle & Grip Strength: Evaluates upper body, core, hips, and grip strength alongside left-and-right balance.
  • Body Composition Check: InBody or DEXA scan at our partner centre, or a centre you already use.
  • Blood Report Review: Helps us understand your internal metabolic health markers.
STEP 02 • DESIGNED FOR YOU

Personalised Training, Nutrition & Lifestyle Plan

built around your data and routine.

Using your assessment results, our team builds a complete programme tailored to your body and daily schedule:

  • Clubhouse Workout Plan: Progressive exercises matched to your clubhouse equipment and joint health.
  • Indian-Kitchen Nutrition: Practical meal guidance that fits your home food with no crash diets.
  • Lifestyle & Recovery Habits: Sleep, mobility, and daily movement routines that support long-term results.
STEP 03 • EVERY WEEK

3 or 4 Coached Sessions a Week in Your Clubhouse

plus optional guided sessions for extra days.

Your Helevate coach comes to your gated community clubhouse every week to coach you through every rep:

  • 4-Day or 3-Day Rhythm: We recommend 4 coached sessions a week, or 3 sessions on Mon, Wed, and Fri.
  • Guided Workout Posters: Want to move on an off day? Your coach shares a simple workout poster to follow on your own.
  • Rest Days Respected: When your body needs recovery, we tell you to rest.
STEP 04 • TWICE A MONTH

Twice-Monthly Progress Reviews & Retesting

keeping your plan on track and us accountable.

We review your progress twice a month and retest regularly so you can see what is really changing:

  • Bi-Weekly Check-Ins: Review workout progression, nutrition adherence, energy, and recovery twice a month.
  • Objective Retesting: Track measurable gains in strength profile, left/right balance, and body composition.
  • Next-Block Adjustments: Update training loads, mobility focus, and nutrition targets based on your results.

Questions

Good to know
before you start.

Where do the sessions happen?

We serve inside your community, usually in your clubhouse gym or your home gym. DM us or call to check whether we already operate in your community.

How many sessions a week?

Every coaching programme is structured around 4 sessions a week, or 3 sessions if you prefer (typically Monday, Wednesday, and Friday).

Can I work out on extra days?

Yes! Your coach provides customized guided posters and recovery guidelines for off days.

What is the strength assessment?

We evaluate your joint mobility, grip strength, core stability, and bilateral balance (checking left versus right imbalances).

Do I need an InBody or DEXA scan?

While not mandatory, it provides an objective baseline of your lean muscle mass, visceral fat, and body composition.

Can I train with an injury?

Absolutely. Our Physio Designed Programmes are specifically structured to rehabilitate painful areas and rebuild joint resilience.

Is this medical treatment?

No, Helevate provides structured exercise coaching, movement rehab, and lifestyle nutrition guidance.
