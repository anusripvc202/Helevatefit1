“The right guidance brought me back to the gym I love.”

Retired Chief Manager, Union Bank

Background: Retired Chief Manager, Union Bank. Back to weight training after a disc problem.

The Coaching Journey: Returning to resistance training after a disc problem requires careful assessment, joint-safe exercise selection, and progressive load management. CLIENT STORY 02 Back Pain Rehab Strength & Mobility
“Rehab focused sessions helped him train with confidence again.”

Rajdeep

Software Professional

Background: Software professional. Long desk hours brought back pain. Rehab focused sessions helped him train with confidence again.

The Coaching Journey: Extended desk hours can create stiffness, core weakness, and persistent lower-back discomfort. Through rehab-focused strengthening sessions in his clubhouse, Rajdeep addressed the root movement imbalances and regained the confidence to train consistently without fear of flare-ups.
