Programme Details

How each service supports your goals.

Every service begins with your baseline assessment and is delivered inside your community clubhouse with regular progress reviews.

01 • FITNESS SESSIONS

Strength Training

built to last.

Coached sessions focused on technique first, then progressive load—building lean muscle while protecting your joints.

  • Upper body, core, hip, and grip strength progression
  • Left-and-right balance checks to correct asymmetries
  • Available as personal or small-group coaching for all levels
Book Strength Consultation
02 • PROGRAMME

Obese to Fit

one steady step at a time.

A supportive, structured pathway for significant fat loss at a pace your joints and metabolism can sustain long term.

  • Tracked with regular InBody or DEXA body composition checks
  • Includes personalised Indian home-food nutrition plans
  • Twice-monthly coach reviews and steady habit accountability
Start Obese to Fit
03 • FITNESS SESSIONS

Performance Training

for the way you play.

Targeted athletic conditioning engineered for runners, recreational sports players, and active adults who want to move faster and stronger.

  • Speed, explosive power, agility, and endurance blocks
  • Single-leg stability and rotational core conditioning
  • Structured workload and recovery management
Book Performance Session
04 • REHAB AND STRENGTHENING

Physio Designed Programmes

back to strong.

Rehabilitation-focused strengthening designed with a physio for residents returning from injury or living with persistent aches.

  • Safe progression for disc problems, back pain, and joint issues
  • Targeted mobility, stability, and posture restoration
  • Guided transition from pain relief back to confident training
Discuss Rehab & Recovery
05 • NUTRITION

Personalised Nutrition

from your own kitchen.

Practical nutrition built around Indian home cooking and your daily family and work routine—with zero crash diets.

  • Practical protein and meal structure using everyday home food
  • Informed by your body composition and blood report review
  • Optional gut and DNA tests through a licensed partner
Personalise Your Nutrition
06 • FOR RESIDENTS

Community Programmes

fitter together.

Group training and wellness programmes run inside your gated community, bringing neighbours of every age together.

  • Coached group sessions inside your apartment clubhouse
  • Scaled exercises suitable for beginners, adults, and seniors
  • Consistent schedule 3 or 4 times a week with your coach
