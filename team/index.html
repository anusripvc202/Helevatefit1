Harish — Health and Performance Coach

“The mind behind Helevate. Harish built Helevate to bring structured, measured coaching into the communities where people live.”

Conventional fitness often relies on guesswork and long commutes to crowded gyms. Harish founded Helevate.fit in Hyderabad with a clear principle: Stronger for longer, measured, not guessed. By bringing certified coaching directly into gated-community clubhouses, residents get the structure of assessment-led training, home-kitchen nutrition, and regular follow-ups right where they live.

ACCREDITATION
IAFM & ACE Certified
Evidence-based health, functional nutrition, and strength coaching standards.
METHODOLOGY
Measured, Not Guessed
Every block starts with strength, balance, and body composition testing.

Four specialisations, working together.

Training, nutrition, rehabilitation, and performance coaching coordinated under one roof inside your community.

SPECIALISATION 01

Strength & Conditioning

functional strength for life.

Building functional strength that carries into everyday life—prioritising joint-safe technique, bilateral balance, and progressive muscle development.

SPECIALISATION 02

Nutrition & Lifestyle

metabolic health & habits.

Sustainable nutrition engineered around metabolic health and Indian home cooking, paired with sleep, hydration, and daily recovery routines.

SPECIALISATION 03

Rehab & Strengthening

pain-free movement.

Targeted mobility and physio-designed rehabilitation for residents recovering from disc problems, back pain, or chronic joint aches.

SPECIALISATION 04

Performance Training

endurance, speed & power.

Data-driven conditioning to elevate endurance, agility, and power for runners, sports players, and active adults.
